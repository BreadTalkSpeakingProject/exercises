01 / Get the gist

Listen once and choose the best answer. Don't worry about individual phrases yet.

02 / Match the meaning

Ten useful expressions connected to the song. Match each with its meaning. Some are short expressions from the lyrics; others are normalized dictionary forms.

03 / Put it in context

Complete each sentence with ONE expression from the bank. Change its grammatical form when necessary. Each expression is used once.

04 / The story

A complete story is waiting for you. Fill in the gaps using the expressions in the bank. Use each once, and pay attention to tense, subject–verb agreement, and pronouns.
