Have you ever…? or Did you…?

Practise present perfect and past simple with football and other fun things. Read the rules first. Then do the tasks in order, from easy to harder. Press Check after each one.

Rules

1. Present perfect: have / has + past participle

We use it for life experience. We do not say when.

Мы используем его для жизненного опыта. Мы не говорим, когда это было.

  • I have seen Messi.
  • She has never played football.
  • Have you ever been to Seoul?
  • Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

Useful words: ever (in questions) and never.

Полезные слова: ever (в вопросах) и never («никогда»).

2. Past simple: verb + -ed / irregular verb

We use it when we know when it happened. The time is finished.

Мы используем его, когда знаем, когда это было. Время уже закончилось.

  • I saw Messi in 2019.
  • We won the match last week.
  • Did you go to Seoul two years ago?
  • When did you "see" that player?

Time words: yesterday, last week, last summer, in 2018, two years ago, when I was ten.

Слова-подсказки: yesterday, last week, last summer, in 2018, two years ago, when I was ten.

3. Use them together

First ask about experience (present perfect). Then ask for details (past simple).

Сначала спрашиваем про опыт (present perfect). Потом спрашиваем детали (past simple).

  • Have you ever won a trophy? → Yes, I have.
  • When did you "win" it? → I won it last year.
