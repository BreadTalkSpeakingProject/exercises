Quick reminder (open if you need it)
  • so + adjective or adverb: so busy, so beautifully.
  • such a / an + (adjective) + singular noun: such a bustling city, such an amazing view.
  • such + (adjective) + plural or uncountable noun: such interesting dishes, such nice weather.
  • so many / so much + noun: so many people, so much noise (not such).
  • so / such … that shows a result: The market was so busy that we couldn't move.
  • Words: take a tour · get to know · avoid the crowds · a wide choice · stay overnight · peace and quiet · packed with people · a bustling place.
