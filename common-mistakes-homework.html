Quick reminder (open if you need it)
  • I don't / he doesn't for habits and facts. I'm … -ing only for "right now". Verbs like enjoy, miss, like, know stay in present simple: "I enjoy the rain", "I miss this time".
  • so + adjective (so long) · such + adjective + noun (such a long vacation).
  • a / an for one thing (a/an issue) · the for something specific · no article in go to school, from Russia, with plural or uncountable nouns.
  • No in with this / next / last + time (this year, next year). We say at night, at 7 a.m., in summer, all day (no during).
  • do = tasks and activities (do a favor, the dishes, homework) · make = create or produce (make a mistake, a decision, a call, money).
  • much + uncountable (information, advice, money, time, homework) · many + countable (problems, lessons).
