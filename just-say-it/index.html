04 / REGISTER

Speak or talk?

Mark each sentence as formal or informal.

a. May I speak to Mr Smith?

b. I was talking with Maria yesterday. You’ll never guess what she told me!

c. Can I talk to you for a second? There is something I wanted to tell you.

d. Dr Johnson will be speaking about the history of Poland.

e. Would you mind speaking more slowly, please?

Complete the rules

3. We use ___ in formal settings, e.g. work.
