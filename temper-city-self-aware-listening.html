SELF AWARE

Temper City · Listening for gist, useful expressions, and controlled practice. All instructions, answers and explanations are in English.

If playback is restricted in your browser, watch the official video on YouTube.

01 · Listening for gist

What's really going on?

Listen once without reading the lyrics. Choose the best answer to each question.

02 · Vocabulary discovery

Six expressions worth keeping

Match each expression to its meaning. Select a definition from each drop-down list.

03 · Controlled practice

Complete the expressions

Type one word in each gap. The sentences are original examples, not song lyrics.

04 · Meaning in context

Choose the best expression

Read each situation and choose the expression that best paraphrases it. Pay attention to meaning, not just individual words.

Teacher's note

All four tasks provide immediate answer explanations after checking. Questions test interpretation rather than requiring learners to reproduce the lyrics. In the gap-fill, the missing word is part of a fixed expression; in the final task, the contexts are paraphrased to test transfer.
