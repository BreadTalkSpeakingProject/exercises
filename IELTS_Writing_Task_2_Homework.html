IELTS Writing Task 2 Homework

Introductions, conclusions and the air-travel essay comparison

Task 6 · Writing tips Task 9 · Matching Compare essays

Introductions and conclusions

DO · Open with the change the prompt describes

“Air travel has become more affordable over time.” Then introduce both views and state your position.

DON’T · Start with a generic “Nowadays” sentence

A broad “Nowadays” sentence uses space without saying what has changed about the cost of air travel.

DO · Give your final judgement

Use “In conclusion” or “To conclude”. Recap the main ideas and restate your view. Save new ideas for the body.

Conclusion openers to avoid in this practice

All in all
Sounds conversational here. “Overall” can express your judgement inside a sentence.
In sum
Can sound abrupt here. Leave space to explain why the trade-off is worth it.
To sum up
Signals a recap. Use “To conclude” here and give your verdict as well as a summary.
Finally
Can introduce one last argument, so the reader may expect another point.
In a nutshell
Sounds conversational here. Use the direct opener modelled above.

Match the introduction frames

Match each introduction frame with its question type. Use each type once.

  1. It is argued that ... This essay agrees/disagrees with that statement because ...
  2. Some say that ... while others would argue that ... This essay will argue that although [view you agree with less], [view you agree with].
  3. This essay will argue that despite [disadvantage], [advantage] means the advantages far outweigh the drawbacks.
  4. This essay will suggest that [problem] ... and the best solution is ...
  5. This essay will argue that [answer 1] and that [answer 2].
What should the first sentence of an introduction never be?

A generic background opening like “Nowadays” or “In the modern world”, or a copy of the question.

What changes in a stronger essay?

Read Essay A before revealing its band. Compare A with B, then B with C. Write your answers and reveal the notes when you’re ready.

1

Read first. Judge from evidence.

Air travel has become cheaper than before. Some think it is a positive development, while others disagree. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Essay A

Nowadays air travel has become cheaper than it used to be. While some people agree that is positive, others believe it is not, and I agree with that.

Some people believe cheaper air travel is beneficial because it allows more people to live a happier life. Firstly, travelling broadens the mind and helps people live a fuller life. People have a chance to learn about different cultures and compare how well they might live. It boosts the economy of countries where people go to. In general, cheaper air travel is a positive development.

Other people think that low cost air travel is a negative trend because it pollutes the atmosphere. More affordable airline tickets allow people to travel more. More planes are going to fly around and the air will be dirtier. This will have a negative effect on the atmosphere and make people less healthy. That is why many believe cheaper air travel is not positive and I agree with them.

In conclusion, cheaper air travel is a bad ideas because it will create more pollution.

Reveal answers
  1. Band 6.0. Essay A addresses both views, gives an opinion and uses a clear paragraph structure.
  2. In body 1, the move to “It boosts the economy of countries where people go to.” diverts from the central claim about a fuller life.
  3. Body 2 has a clearer chain: cheaper tickets → more travel → more planes → dirtier air → poorer health.
  4. Clear paragraphing and a stated position show basic control. The limited development of ideas, mostly basic vocabulary and limited sentence variety keep the response at Band 6.
2

A → B · Introduction and position

Essay A

Nowadays air travel has become cheaper than it used to be. While some people agree that is positive, others believe it is not, and I agree with that.

Reveal Essay B

These days travelling by a plane has become more affordable than it used to be. While some people believe it is a positive trend as it will make people live fuller live, I agree with those who are sure that it is a negative development because cheaper flights will cause harm to environment.

Reveal answers
  1. Band 7.0. A states an opinion. B also tells the reader why the writer holds that opinion, pointing to environmental harm.
  2. B links the positive view to a fuller life and the negative view to environmental harm, identifying the writer’s side. The improvement is a position with arguments, beyond “I agree”.
3

A → B · Body 1: develop one claim

Essay A

Some people believe cheaper air travel is beneficial because it allows more people to live a happier life. Firstly, travelling broadens the mind and helps people live a fuller life. People have a chance to learn about different cultures and compare how well they might live. It boosts the economy of countries where people go to. In general, cheaper air travel is a positive development.

Reveal Essay B

Some people believe that less expensive air travel is beneficial, since it will provide more people with an opportunity to live happier, fuller life. It is conventional wisdom that the more diverse experiences a person has, the happier they are. Of course, travel can be quite a bright experience in a person's lifetime, however, few people have a chance to travel. Thus, with more affordable air travel, more people have a chance to fill their lives with unforgettable moments, which will make them happier.

Reveal answers
  1. A shifts from a fuller life to the economy. B sustains the connection between diverse experiences, wider access to travel and happiness.
  2. “Conventional wisdom” introduces the idea that diverse experiences make people happier. “Unforgettable moments” shows what travel can offer, and the final relative clause links those moments to happiness.
4

A → B · Body 2: make causation explicit

Essay A

Other people think that low cost air travel is a negative trend because it pollutes the atmosphere. More affordable airline tickets allow people to travel more. More planes are going to fly around and the air will be dirtier. This will have a negative effect on the atmosphere and make people less healthy. That is why many believe cheaper air travel is not positive and I agree with them.

Reveal Essay B

Other people believe that cheaper air travel is a negative development, since more planes will cause more polluting exhaust fumes, and I agree with that. Cheaper air travel will increase the demand, which means more planes will fly around. This in turn will increase the amount of exhaust fumes in the air, which will have a devastating effect on our atmosphere. That is why many believe that affordability of air travel is not beneficial, and I share the same viewpoint.

Reveal answers
  1. B: cheaper travel → higher demand → more flights → more exhaust fumes → atmospheric damage.
  2. “Increase the demand”, “exhaust fumes” and “devastating effect” give B more precise language. “This in turn” makes the next step explicit. B relies heavily on these signposts and uses relatively little reference or substitution.
5

A → B · Conclusion: close the argument

Essay A

In conclusion, cheaper air travel is a bad ideas because it will create more pollution.

Reveal Essay B

In conclusion, while some people are sure cheaper air travel is beneficial since it will make many much happier, I agree with those who argue that it will have a negative effect on the environment.

Reveal answers
  1. B’s conclusion returns to the same logic as its introduction. Cheaper flights can make life fuller, but the writer gives more weight to environmental harm.
  2. B gives reasons for the writer’s position and develops them through clearer steps. Its more precise vocabulary and varied grammar help the reader follow those steps.
6

B → C · Introduction and position

Essay B

These days travelling by a plane has become more affordable than it used to be. While some people believe it is a positive trend as it will make people live fuller live, I agree with those who are sure that it is a negative development because cheaper flights will cause harm to environment.

Reveal Essay C

Travelling by air has become much more affordable than it used to be in the past. While some are sure it is a positive trend since it may give many a chance to lead a richer, more satisfying way of life, I side with those who insist on cheaper air travel being a negative development, as the irreparable harm will be done not only to the environment, but people as well.

Reveal answers
  1. Band 8.0. C presents the benefit of a richer life and extends the objection to harm to both the environment and people. That gives the essay two consequences to develop.
  2. “richer, more satisfying way of life” illustrates vocabulary supporting the argument, beyond a simple positive/negative contrast.
7

B → C · Body 1: acknowledge, then weigh

Essay B

Some people believe that less expensive air travel is beneficial, since it will provide more people with an opportunity to live happier, fuller life. It is conventional wisdom that the more diverse experiences a person has, the happier they are. Of course, travel can be quite a bright experience in a person's lifetime, however, few people have a chance to travel. Thus, with more affordable air travel, more people have a chance to fill their lives with unforgettable moments, which will make them happier.

Reveal Essay C

Some people believe that the reduction in the price of airline tickets is a positive trend as it will allow more people to live a more eventful life, thus contributing to the overall satisfaction with life. Due to high costs of air travel in the past, many people were deprived of the opportunity to see a variety of places, which resulted in them leading a much less colourful, more monotonous life. Since the situation has changed, more people now have an opportunity to see the world and make their lives filled with adventures. However, these newly-obtained opportunities pale in comparison with the repercussions the growing travel industry will have on other aspects of our lives.

Reveal answers
  1. C develops the benefit by showing how it came about. High fares once kept more people from travelling. Lower fares give more people opportunities to see new places and have a more eventful life.
  2. “These newly-obtained opportunities pale in comparison with the repercussions” acknowledges the benefit and shows how the writer weighs it. The costs matter more in the writer’s judgement.
  3. “These newly-obtained opportunities” refers to the chance to see the world and have more varied experiences. That phrase carries the idea into the writer’s judgement.
  4. “Deprived of the opportunity” shows how high fares limited access to travel. “Pale in comparison” tells the reader how little weight the writer gives the benefit relative to the costs.
8

B → C · Body 2: connect cost and consequence

Essay B

Other people believe that cheaper air travel is a negative development, since more planes will cause more polluting exhaust fumes, and I agree with that. Cheaper air travel will increase the demand, which means more planes will fly around. This in turn will increase the amount of exhaust fumes in the air, which will have a devastating effect on our atmosphere. That is why many believe that affordability of air travel is not beneficial, and I share the same viewpoint.

Reveal Essay C

Other people believe that the decrease in the cost of air travel is more of a negative tendency, since it will add to the pollution problem, harming the environment and, consequently, the people. With dropping prices, demand for air travel is likely to grow, thus causing more exhaust fumes to be emitted as a result of the increasingly growing number of flights. This is bound to take its toll on the condition of the environment, which in turn will have a detrimental effect on the health and well-being of many people, thus rendering the lower air travel costs a disadvantage. Many are assured that those problems are a big price to pay for the opportunity to make one’s life more eventful, and I side with this viewpoint.

Reveal answers
  1. C extends the cost to health and well-being, then weighs it against a more eventful life.
  2. “This” picks up the rise in flights and exhaust fumes. “Those problems” refers to environmental damage and its effects on people. “This viewpoint” refers to the judgement that those costs outweigh the benefit of a more eventful life.
  3. The participle clause “thus causing more exhaust fumes to be emitted” gives a consequence. The relative clause “which in turn will have a detrimental effect” links environmental damage to health. “Is likely to grow” presents higher demand as probable.
9

B → C · Conclusion and synthesis

Essay B

In conclusion, while some people are sure cheaper air travel is beneficial since it will make many much happier, I agree with those who argue that it will have a negative effect on the environment.

Reveal Essay C

In conclusion, although many people believe lowering prices of airline tickets is a benefit to society since that will allow people to live a more joyous, fulfilled life, I stand with those who argue that it will cause environmental problems which in turn may trigger health-related issues, thus making reduced air travel cost a negative development.

Reveal answers
  1. C’s conclusion returns to the same trade-off developed in the introduction and body. Cheaper flights can make life more fulfilling, but the writer gives greater weight to environmental damage and the health problems it can cause.
  2. C adds a human consequence, “health and well-being”. It develops that idea by tracing the steps from lower prices to more flights, more fumes and environmental damage. The phrase “which in turn” connects the environmental damage to its effect on people.
  3. Linking words help readers follow a relationship that the writer has already explained. In C, “which in turn” works because the paragraph has already shown how more flights can lead to environmental damage.
  4. Choose two principles based on what you noticed in the essays. For example, trace a cause through to its consequence and show why one side carries more weight. In your next essay, check whether each step is clear enough for a reader to follow.
