Today’s rules (cheat sheet)

  • A repeated past: We would go to the sea every summer.
  • B imaginary situation: If I lived in New York, I would swim every day.
  • C soften an opinion: I wouldn’t say that. / I would say…
  • D future in the past: I thought it would be easy.
  • E refusal / failure: My laptop wouldn’t start.
  • F polite want: I would like a coffee, please.
  • After would, a when-clause goes in the past: It would be sunny when I woke up.
  • Two full sentences need and / a full stop, not just a comma. Say go for a walk, go for a bike ride.
