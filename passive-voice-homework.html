Part 1. The rules

Read the rules first. Then look at the examples.

  1. Passive voice shows the action, not the doer. We use it when the doer is not known, not important, or when the action or the result is more important.
  2. The formula is: subject + to be + V3. V3 is the past participle: sold, written, chosen, opened. V3 never changes. Only the form of to be changes.
  3. The tense is shown by to be. Present Simple: am / is / are. Past Simple: was / were. Future Simple: will be.
  4. To be agrees with the subject of the passive sentence. That is the thing that receives the action, not the doer. One subject: is / was. Many subjects: are / were. I: am / was. Will be is the same for every subject.
  5. Use by + doer only when the doer is important or interesting. If it is not important, leave it out.
  6. Negative and questions use to be only. Negative: is not paid, were not sold, will not be opened. Question: Is it paid? Were they sold? Will it be opened?
  7. Only verbs with an object have a passive. The CEO announced the plan becomes The plan was announced. Verbs with no object cannot be passive: The turnover increased. The price dropped. The sales grew. It consists of ten people.

Part 2. The rules with examples

In every pair, the blue part is the subject, and the green part is the verb.

Present Simple
am / is / are + V3
Active
The company sells its products online.
Passive
Its products are sold online.
Negative / question
They are not sold in shops.
Are they sold in shops?
Past Simple
was / were + V3
Active
The CEO announced the results.
Passive
The results were announced by the CEO.
Negative / question
The rumor was not believed.
Was the rumor believed?
Future Simple
will be + V3
Active
The board will choose a new CEO.
Passive
A new CEO will be chosen by the board.
Negative / question
It will not be opened in May.
Will it be opened in May?

Agreement: look at the subject, not at the doer

The report is written by two analysts. (one report → is)

The reports are written by the CEO. (many reports → are, even if the CEO is one person)

The workforce is paid on the 5th. (workforce is singular here → is)

I am paid on the 10th. (I → am)

No passive for verbs without an object

The share price dropped slightly. It is active. We cannot say was dropped here.
